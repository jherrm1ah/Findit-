The Admin Handbook

What every tab in the Admin Queue actually does, what it deliberately does not do, and the five distinctions that trip up every new administrator.

Your first run through

These six are a real sequence. Each one unblocks the next, so work them in order before you invite anyone else in.

  1. Approve your own seller account

    Open Sellers, find your account under Seller accounts, set it to Approved. Until you do, you cannot list a product, send an offer, or upload an image, because pending genuinely blocks all three.

  2. Set the platform fee

    Open Payments and set the percentage FindIt keeps on each order. Do this before your first real sale. The fee is frozen onto each order at the moment it is paid, so orders already paid keep the old rate forever.

  3. Check your plan prices

    Open Plans. Every Store tier and FindIt Pro is an editable card, and boost prices sit on the same tab. Prices live in the database, not in code, so nothing here needs a deploy.

  4. Shape your categories

    Open Categories. Create the ones you want sellers to list under and deactivate the ones you do not. Buyers and sellers see this list immediately.

  5. Add your team

    Open Admin tools, look a person up by phone number, and promote them with the narrowest role that fits their job. Only a super admin sees this tab.

  6. Then work from Alerts every day

    Alerts is the daily driver. It gathers every count that needs a human decision and each one clicks straight through to the tab that owns it.

The fourteen tabs

Which tabs you see depends on your admin role. The label on each says who it appears for.

OverviewEvery admin

Live counts across sellers, verification, users and money, each tile clickable through to the tab that owns it. Includes monthly recurring revenue and the last thirty days of cancellations and expirations.

Not: a projection. Every figure is counted from real rows at the moment you load it.

AlertsEvery admin

A single "what needs attention" queue. Failed items, pending verifications, open disputes and anything else waiting on a decision.

Not: a new signal. Everything here already exists on its own tab. This is the shortcut, not a separate system.

SellersModeration

Two jobs. Reported problems, where a buyer has raised an issue and you decide refund or release. And Seller accounts, where you move a seller between pending, approved, rejected and suspended.

This is the gate. Only Approved sellers can list, send offers, or upload images.

VerificationVerification

Trust submissions from the seller onboarding wizard, with their evidence photos served as short-lived signed links. Approving here drives the public New, Verified and Trusted badge.

Not: the selling gate. A verification decision never grants or removes the right to sell. That is the Sellers tab.

UsersSupport

Every account on the platform, searchable and filterable by role. Suspend or reactivate an account outright.

Not: the same as seller suspension. Suspending here locks the whole account, buyer side included.

PaymentsFinance

The platform fee configuration, and the seller payout queue with the ability to mark a payout settled.

Forward only. Changing the fee never rewrites an order that was already paid.

PlansFinance

The editor for every subscription plan, Store tiers and FindIt Pro alike, plus boost pricing. Name, price, limits, features and active state.

Not: retroactive. An active subscriber keeps what they are on until it renews.

AnalyticsFinance

Real daily totals for orders, users and sellers over seven, thirty or ninety days.

Not: a forecast. There is no projected trend anywhere in this tab.

CategoriesModeration

Create, rename, activate and deactivate the categories sellers list under.

Safe to edit. Deactivating a category leaves existing listings alone. It only stops new ones choosing it.

RiskModeration

Sellers whose real dispute rate warrants a look, computed from actual order outcomes.

Not: an accusation or a score. A seller needs at least three real orders before any rate appears at all.

SupportSupport

Real tickets raised from Help and support inside the app. Reply in thread and mark resolved.

Reopening is automatic. If the user replies to a resolved ticket, it reopens itself.

RequestsEvery admin

Buyer requests that no seller has offered on yet. Your supply gap, in one list.

Not: actionable in place. It tells you where demand is going unmet.

Admin toolsSuper admin

Promote and demote administrators by phone number, run the seller identity migration, and send a broadcast notification to every matching account.

Broadcast is real. It sends immediately, is rate limited, and respects each recipient's own notification preference.

ActivitySuper admin

The audit log of every admin action with who did it and when, plus verification code statistics over the recent window.

Append only. Nothing in this log can be edited from the interface.

Who sees what

Roles are enforced on the server, not by hiding buttons. A restricted admin calling an endpoint directly is refused the same way.

Overview, Alerts and Requests are shared. Every admin sees those three whatever their role.

Five distinctions worth learning once

Nearly every mistake a new administrator makes comes from conflating one of these pairs.

Seller status is not the verification badge

Status on the Sellers tab decides whether someone can sell at all. Verification decides what badge buyers see next to their name. Approving a verification does not let a pending seller list anything.

Suspending a user is not suspending a seller

The Users tab locks an entire account, so the person cannot buy either. The Sellers tab removes selling privileges while leaving the rest of their account working.

A fee change never reaches the past

The rate is stamped onto each order the moment payment is confirmed. Drop the fee tomorrow and every order paid today still reports what it actually charged.

Trusted cannot be granted

It needs admin approval plus a real record: at least ten completed orders, no disputes ever, and a rating of four or above where ratings exist. There is no button that makes someone Trusted.

Marking a payout paid does not move money

With no payment provider configured, payouts land as manual required. Marking one paid records that you sent the money outside FindIt. The system never claims a transfer happened when none did.

Not yet connected

These are built in code and waiting on an external account or key. Until each is configured, the matching feature reports that it is unavailable rather than pretending to work.
